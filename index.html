How do I book a consignment?
Call our dispatch line, message us on WhatsApp, or fill in the quote form on this page with your route and load details. We'll come back with a plan and a price, usually within one business day.
Can I track my shipment while it's on the road?
Yes. Every vehicle in our fleet carries GPS tracking, so we can give you the current position and expected arrival whenever you call. Tracking is included on every consignment, not charged as an add-on.
What size loads can you carry?
Our multi-axle trucks carry between 16 and 30 tons depending on the class of vehicle, and larger consignments can be split across several vehicles running the same lane.
Which routes do you operate?
Our regular corridors run from Kolhapur to Pune, Hyderabad, Delhi, Chennai and Kolkata. We also take on new lanes for regular contract freight — get in touch with the route you need.
